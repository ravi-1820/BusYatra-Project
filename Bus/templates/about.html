Our Mission

Making Travel Accessible for Every Indian

BusYatra was founded with one simple goal — to make long-distance travel in India affordable, safe, and stress-free. We believe every person deserves the right to travel comfortably, regardless of their budget.

We partner with hundreds of bus operators across the country, providing passengers with verified schedules, real-time seat availability, instant booking confirmations, and 24/7 customer support.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These principles guide every decision we make — from the operators we partner with to the features we build.

Safety First

We rigorously vet every bus operator, check safety records, and only partner with those who meet our high standards for passenger safety.

Full Transparency

No hidden charges. No surprise fees. What you see is what you pay — always. We believe in complete honesty with our customers.

Innovation

We continuously improve our platform with smarter search, real-time tracking, instant refunds, and features built based on traveller feedback.

Customer First

Every feature, every policy, every update starts with one question: "Does this make travel better for our customers?"

Inclusivity

We design for all Indians — in multiple languages, for all screen sizes, and with accessibility at the heart of our product design.

Sustainability

100% digital, paperless ticketing reduces our environmental footprint. We actively promote shared travel over individual transport.

Our Story

The BusYatra Journey

From a small startup in Bangalore to India's most loved bus booking platform — here's how we grew.

18
2018 — The Beginning
BusYatra launched with just 12 bus operators and 3 routes. The idea was simple: one app for all your bus bookings.
19
2019 — First 1 Lakh Users
Within a year, we reached 1 lakh registered users and expanded to 50+ routes across South India.
21
2021 — Pan-India Expansion
Post-pandemic, we expanded to 500+ routes and launched our digital QR ticketing system, eliminating paper tickets entirely.
23
2023 — 5 Million Travellers
We crossed the 5 million traveller milestone and launched our Operator Manager Portal, empowering bus companies to manage their fleet digitally.
26
2026 — Today & Beyond
With 800+ routes, 200+ operators, and a 4.9-star average rating, BusYatra continues to redefine bus travel in India.
