Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Find quick answers to the most common queries before contacting us.

How do I cancel my bus ticket?
Go to My Orders from your profile, select the booking you want to cancel, and click "Cancel Booking". Refunds are processed within 3-5 working days depending on the operator's cancellation policy.
I didn't receive my ticket. What should I do?
Check your registered email inbox (including the spam folder). You can also find your ticket under My Orders in your account. If still not found, contact our support team with your booking details.
How long does a refund take?
Refunds to your original payment method take 3-5 working days for net banking/cards, and are instant if credited to your BusYatra Wallet. Wallet refunds can be used for future bookings.
