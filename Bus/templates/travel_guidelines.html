Essential Travel Policies & Guidelines

Important instructions to note before and during your journey

Before You Travel

  • Reach boarding point at least 15–30 minutes before departure.
  • Carry a valid Government ID.
  • Keep your e-ticket ready.
  • Verify boarding location before departure.
  • Keep your phone charged.

Baggage Policy

  • One medium luggage allowed.
  • Keep valuables with yourself.
  • Do not carry prohibited items.
  • BusYatra is not responsible for lost luggage.

During the Journey

  • Follow driver instructions.
  • Keep bus clean.
  • Respect fellow passengers.
  • Smoking prohibited.
  • Alcohol prohibited.

Women's Safety

  • Travel with verified operators.
  • Share live location with family.
  • Contact support whenever required.

Health & Hygiene

  • Maintain hygiene.
  • Carry personal medicines.
  • Report any emergency immediately.

Cancellation & Refund

  • Cancellation depends upon operator policy.
  • Refund is processed through original payment method.
  • Convenience fee may not be refundable.
